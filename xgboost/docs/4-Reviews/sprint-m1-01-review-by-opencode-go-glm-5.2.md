## 1. Executive summary

The requirement and the first sprint plan are well-constructed: MC-only scope, frozen protocol-driven configuration, physical development/test separation, eligible-only model publication, one-shot test opening, and no-clobber artifact semantics all match the approved design, and the qualification thresholds (weighted OOF AUC `>= 0.80`, three ZZ KS `<= 0.10`, signal efficiency strictly above achieved background efficiency) are identical to the frozen policy in `AGENTS.md`.

Before execution, the following must be addressed:

1. **[H-1]** `xgboost/AGENTS.md` — the mandated first-read governance document — still declares the next stage to be decorrelation-aware training research and has not been updated to record the user's approval of the 2026-09-01 refactor design. This governing-source conflict must be reconciled.
2. **[H-2]** Sprint M1-01's "固定数值精度政策" task does not specify the policy's required content — golden-baseline platform authority and per-value-class rules. Project history (R2 stopped over a `9.66e-13` cross-platform float difference; R3 introduced no tolerance) makes this decisive, and the current worktree is Windows while the reference environment is macOS ARM64.
3. **[M-1..M-7]** Design-stage-1 evidence tasks were dropped in the merge into M1-01; two acceptance criteria lack matching verification commands (installability/console scripts; protocol transcription fidelity); the characterization baseline covers only a subset of design §13.1; the flat test layout conflicts with the design target structure and later sprints' focused verification; the per-sprint review-confirm evidence required by FR-001 is not enumerated; and `AGENTS.md` is missing from M1-05's documentation-update scope.
4. **[L-1..L-5, I-1..I-4]** Minor precision, traceability, and platform-specific items.

No scientific-safety violation was found in either target document.

## 5. Positive observations

- **Scientific safety is fully preserved.** Both targets keep the MC-only scope (DSID 345060/363490), define no real-data input type, forbid `m4l`/identifiers/provenance/weight fields in the model, keep gates identical to `AGENTS.md` (AUC `>= 0.80`, three KS `<= 0.10`, signal efficiency strictly above achieved background efficiency), treat `no_eligible_candidate` as a normal terminal with no model and no test-opening, enforce one-shot atomic claims, and use no-clobber runs with failure receipts.
- **Sprint scope is appropriately narrow.** M1-01 avoids migrating preprocessing/training or deleting history, and its risk controls ("不移动现有科学函数", "不读取任何真实数据或冻结 run") match the design's characterization-first approach.
- **The scientific freeze is consistent everywhere.** Fixed 19-feature order, single candidate, common training parameters, and three working points agree across FR-001, the design, and the sprint set.
- **The transcription task is executable as written.** All design-named V1 authorities exist in the repository (verified), and `src/config.py`/`src/cli/`/`src/artifacts/` do not collide with existing modules.
- **Failure and degradation semantics are clear.** Fail-closed on mismatch, no implicit repair, no gate downgrade, synthetic-fixture fallback without claiming authoritative reproduction, and blocking sprint gates are all explicitly stated in FR-001.
