## Summary verdict

**Accept with required doc-sync revisions.** Every one of the 30 accepted findings from the first M1-05 review cycle (GLM F01–F16; Kimi P1–P5/M1–M2/L1–L7 via the confirm table) is verified incorporated in the revised documents, including claim/receipt durability (fsync before decode), the fixed post-claim exit mapping, the hash-bound protocol snapshot as the sole runtime scientific source, sanitized `failure.json`, the preprocess-run re-binding rule, unparseable/empty/partial state permanence, missing-flag-vs-blank-value exits, `split==test`, orphan-staging semantics, pre-claim abort discipline, dual test-count exact equality, tree-only-state mutation, and the root-design layout/module-tree syncs. The three newly added rules are reviewed in depth: the conservative empty-selected-background KS rule is correct, fail-closed, and already consistently implemented and unit-tested; authorization-reference hygiene is honest and mostly pinned; terminal-receipt durability failure semantics are specified but incompletely wired into the stable-exit table and test gate. No Critical or High findings remain. Three Medium findings (§6 nonreproduction catch-all vs §8 exit mapping; unspecified parent-directory flush for the newly created `state/`; two exit-4 classes missing from §8) and five Low findings should be fixed in the documents — most are already embodied by the in-flight implementation and are therefore doc-side syncs that should land before the M1-05 code-review gate.
