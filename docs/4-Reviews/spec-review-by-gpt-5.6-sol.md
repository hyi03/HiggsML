# Independent Document Review: H4l off-only marginal CRN v3

## Review scope

- Reviewed artifact: `docs/changes/h4l-off-marginal-coupling-v3/spec.md`
- Review type: document/specification review
- Evidence consulted: repository guidance and maintained H4l methods/results documentation; the current v2 J0 artifact at `runs/h4l-off-test01-v2/support-j0/joint-support-summary.json`; and the directly relevant v2 support, RNG, assessment, T2, schema, and workflow code.
- Evidence boundary: static review plus inspection of an existing controlled-MC J0 artifact. No tests or new scientific runs were executed.

## Summary

The common-total monotone construction is mathematically capable of preserving each candidate's per-process, per-mass-bin two-category Poisson law. The draft also correctly separates that artificial CRN dependence from physical-event pairing and preserves the assessment-history boundary. Approval should nevertheless wait for the requirements below to be made decision-complete: as written, an implementation could pass the stated marginal and determinism tests while violating the full independent-bin observation law, could overstate legacy label-level evidence as process-level support, and could publish inaccurate T2 budget accounting after a late outer-replica failure.

## Positive observations

- The draft correctly introduces v3 instead of changing the meaning of immutable v2 evidence.
- The marginal Poisson-splitting derivation is sound for one process/mass-bin cell when its rates are finite and nonnegative.
- The assessment-history, freeze-before-access, signed-rate rejection, fresh-run-root, and evidence-level boundaries are explicit and consistent with repository policy.
- The current J0 characterization values quoted in the problem statement were confirmed at the aggregate-label level: five failed seed blocks, 2,238-2,522 cells, 62 positive projected entries per seed, zero projection mismatches, and no assessment payload read.

## Conclusion

**Recommendation: revise before approval.** There are 6 actionable findings: 4 High and 2 Medium. The central construction can proceed after the stream independence, evidence qualification, T2 accounting, and default-cutover requirements are made explicit.
