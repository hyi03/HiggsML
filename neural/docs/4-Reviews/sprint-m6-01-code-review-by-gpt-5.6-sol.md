## Final assessment

The change set should not be accepted as M6-complete yet. The minimum blocking work is to make disabled execution genuinely zero-decode, publish and replay the complete capacity/CDF artifact chains, prove exclusion-set coverage before claim creation, verify evaluation receipts, and harden the confirmation reader and preclaim grammar boundary.
